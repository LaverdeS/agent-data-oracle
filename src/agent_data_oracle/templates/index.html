Conditional evidence

Results are not legal advice or a determination that a product is recalled, safe, compliant, illegal, removable, cleared, or permitted for sale. No-candidate results do not prove completeness.

Strictly bounded data

The service uses only official CPSC recall records and identifiers you paste. It does not crawl pages or accept catalogues, files, customer, order, or marketplace data.

No payment

No payment is accepted during this usage-learning phase. Free activity does not establish willingness to pay, product-market fit, or a data moat.

Privacy and providers

Operator submissions and evaluations are retained only through the phase and no later than 30 days after the phase closes, subject to valid earlier deletion or a documented incident hold. Application and security logs are retained for 14 days and exclude submitted identifiers and email content.

Application data is hosted on Google Cloud in Frankfurt. Sign-in email is processed by Google Workspace outside that regional boundary. GitHub is used for development and CI without production data. Public CPSC requests contain no operator submission.

This independent service is not endorsed by CPSC.
